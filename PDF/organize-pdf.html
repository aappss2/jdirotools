The Complete Guide to Converting Images to PDF

Converting images to PDF is one of the most common tasks in digital document management. Whether you're compiling scanned documents, creating a photo portfolio, or organizing receipts, having a reliable image to PDF converter saves time and effort.

Why Convert Images to PDF?

PDF (Portable Document Format) is the universal standard for document sharing. Unlike image files that may display differently across devices, PDFs maintain consistent formatting everywhere. Converting your JPG to PDF or PNG to PDF ensures your documents look professional and are easy to share.

Benefits of Client-Side Processing

Our tool processes everything directly in your web browser using JavaScript. This means your images are never uploaded to any server, providing maximum privacy and security. It also means faster processing since there's no upload/download time involved.

Supported Image Formats

  • JPEG/JPG — The most common image format, ideal for photographs
  • PNG — Supports transparency, great for screenshots and graphics
  • WEBP — Modern format with excellent compression
  • BMP — Uncompressed bitmap format
  • GIF — Supports simple graphics (first frame used)

Tips for Best Results

For the highest quality PDFs, use high-resolution images and select the appropriate page size. The "Fit to Image" option adapts each PDF page to match the exact dimensions of your images, preventing any cropping or stretching.
