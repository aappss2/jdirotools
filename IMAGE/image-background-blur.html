Free Image Background Blur Tool — Create Portrait-Style Blur Online

Our Image Background Blur Tool makes it easy to blur the background of any photo while keeping your subject crystal clear. Simply upload your picture, paint over the subject, and download the result as PNG or JPG. Everything runs in your browser — no uploads, no signup.

Why blur the background?

  • Focus attention on people or products
  • Create a professional portrait or bokeh look
  • Hide distracting backgrounds for social media
  • Improve readability in thumbnails and banners

How it works

  1. Upload your image.
  2. Set Blur Strength (subtle to strong).
  3. Use the brush to mark the subject you want to keep sharp (white mask).
  4. Toggle Erase to fix edges, then download as PNG or JPG.
