Why use a social media resizer?

Every platform has its own dimensions. With J1RO’s resizer, you can produce all major sizes in one go — no guessing, no repetitive cropping. It’s perfect for creators, marketers, and brands who want consistent, high-quality visuals everywhere.

How it works

  1. Upload one image (or load the sample).
  2. Select the outputs you need (or use presets like Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube).
  3. Drag inside a tile to pan; scroll/pinch to zoom. Use Cover (fill) or Contain (pad).
  4. Toggle Safe Areas to keep text/logos inside platform UI zones.
  5. Choose format (JPEG/PNG/WEBP), quality, and base filename.
  6. Download any single size or hit Download All (ZIP).

Best practices for safe compositions

  • Keep text and logos within the safe-area guides (especially Stories and YouTube banners).
  • Use Cover for edge-to-edge visuals; use Contain with a brand background color for exact framing.
  • Start from a high-resolution source to preserve sharpness across sizes.
  • For profile images, center the subject within the circular preview guide.

Export settings

  • JPEG: Best for photos — small file size with adjustable quality.
  • WEBP: Great compression and quality; widely supported.
  • PNG: Lossless; use for graphics or when you need crisp edges.

Privacy & performance

Everything runs locally in your browser. Your images never leave your device. The tool uses high-quality canvas rendering and exports a ZIP when you download all sizes.

FAQ: Social Media Image Resizer

Does this upload my images to a server?

No. All processing is client‑side in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

How do I keep text/logos from being cut off?

Enable Show Safe Areas. Keep critical elements inside the dotted guide (especially for Stories and YouTube banners).

Cover vs. Contain — which should I use?

Cover fills the frame (may crop edges). Contain fits the entire image and adds padding with your chosen background color.

What formats are best?

JPEG or WEBP for photos (smaller files). PNG for graphics, logos, or when you want lossless edges.

“Download All” isn’t working — why?

If you dragged an online image, the browser may block export (tainted canvas). Use the Choose Image button (local file) or Load Sample.

Can I adjust the crop for each size?

Yes. Drag to pan and scroll/pinch to zoom in each tile. Double‑click a tile to reset its framing.

Do you support more sizes?

This tool covers the most-used sizes. If you need a specific dimension, let us know and we can add it.
