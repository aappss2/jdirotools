How to Make the Perfect Profile Picture

  1. Upload your photo (or enter initials).

Tips for Better Results

  • Use bright, even lighting. Avoid strong backlit scenes.
  • Leave some space above the head to prevent tight crops.
  • Pick background colors with contrast to your clothes/hair.
