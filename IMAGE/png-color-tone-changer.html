PNG Color Tone Changer: Fast Online Color Grading

Fine‑tune your image with professional‑style controls — hue, saturation, vibrance, lightness, brightness, contrast, gamma, temperature, tint, and colorize. Mask by luminance range and preview a mask overlay. Everything runs locally in your browser.

What you can do

  • Adjust Hue (global color rotation) and Saturation/Vibrance for punchier colors
  • Control Lightness, Brightness, Contrast, and Gamma for tonal balance
  • Set Temperature (cool ↔ warm) and Tint (green ↔ magenta)
  • Apply a Colorize tint with adjustable amount for duotone/brand styles
  • Limit effects to shadows/mids/highlights with a Luminance range mask + softness
  • Use Presets (Warm, Cool, Teal & Orange, Sepia, B&W, Vivid, Pastel, Filmic)

How to use

  1. Upload an image or load the sample.
  2. Pick a Preset or adjust controls (Hue, Sat, Vibrance, etc.).
  3. Optionally enable Colorize, set the tint color, and amount.
  4. Enable Apply Range to restrict changes by luminance (Min/Max/Softness) and toggle Mask overlay.
  5. Hold O or click Show Original to compare.
  6. Download PNG when you’re happy.

Control reference

  • Hue: rotates colors around the wheel (−180° to +180°)
  • Saturation: increases all color intensity; Vibrance focuses on muted areas
  • Lightness: shifts midtones (HSL); Brightness: overall exposure
  • Contrast: expands or compresses tonal range
  • Gamma: mid‑tone curve (values <1 brighten mids, >1 darken)
  • Temperature: blue ↔ orange; Tint: green ↔ magenta
  • Colorize: applies a chosen hue/sat while preserving luminance
  • Range mask: Min/Max luminance with Softness feathering

Tips for great results

  • Use Vibrance instead of high Saturation to avoid oversaturation.
  • Fine‑tune Gamma after Brightness/Contrast for cleaner mids.
  • Restrict edits to shadows or highlights with the range mask for cinematic looks.
  • Combine Temperature + Tint to fix white balance issues fast.

Privacy & performance

Your image never leaves your device. The tool processes pixels in your browser for instant feedback and watermark‑free PNG export.

FAQ: PNG Color Tone Changer

What’s the difference between Brightness and Lightness?

Brightness shifts overall exposure in RGB, while Lightness (HSL) targets midtones more gently and preserves some contrast.

Vibrance vs Saturation?

Saturation boosts all colors uniformly; Vibrance boosts muted colors more than already‑vivid ones to avoid clipping skin tones and highlights.

What do Temperature and Tint do?

Temperature pushes blue ↔ orange; Tint pushes green ↔ magenta. Together they correct white balance or set mood.

How does Colorize work?

It applies the chosen hue/saturation while preserving each pixel’s luminance for natural light/dark structure. Increase Amount for a stronger duotone effect.

What is the Luminance range mask?

It limits adjustments to a brightness range: set Min/Max and soften edges with Softness. Great for “edit only shadows” or “only highlights.”

Why is export blocked sometimes?

If you drag an image from another website, the browser may mark the canvas as cross‑origin (“tainted”). Use local files or the built‑in sample.

Does this upload my images?

No. All processing is client‑side; images never leave your device.
