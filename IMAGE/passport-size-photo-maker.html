Passport Size Photo Maker — Free Online Tool

This free Passport Size Photo Maker helps you create compliant passport photos in minutes. Upload your picture, adjust the crop with zoom and drag, choose a standard size (such as 35×45 mm or 2×2 in), set 300 DPI, and export a single photo or a printable multi-photo sheet on A4, Letter, or 4×6 paper.

Key Features

  • Standard passport sizes (35×45 mm, 2×2 in, 50×50 mm, and more)
  • Drag to pan, slider to zoom, locked aspect crop
  • Background fill: white / light blue / gray / transparent
  • Print-ready at chosen DPI (300 recommended)
  • Generate multi-photo sheets with margins and gutters

Tips for Acceptance

  • Use a recent photo with plain background, even lighting, and neutral expression
  • Face the camera directly; avoid shadows and accessories that obscure features
  • Verify exact specifications (size, head height, eye level, background) for your country
