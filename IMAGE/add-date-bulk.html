How to Add Date to Photos Online in Bulk — A Practical Guide

Add dates and timestamps to large batches of images quickly, with options for style, format and extra whitespace at the bottom for print or archival needs.

Adding dates to photos is a basic yet crucial step when organizing photos for documentation, legal records, property management, construction progress, or personal archives. Whether you’re a contractor who needs a dated photo log, a parent making a physical album, or a business archiving evidence, stamping a date onto many images by hand is tedious. This tool from JDIRO solves that — you can upload dozens or hundreds of images at once, choose how the date is shown, add optional white space at the bottom (useful for printing or captions), preview all outputs, and download them individually or as a compressed ZIP.

Bulk date-stamping reduces administrative time and enforces consistency. Built with privacy in mind, the entire process happens in your browser: images do not leave your computer unless you choose to download them. Use the “Today’s date” option to apply the current date automatically, or select “Custom date” to use a specific date for all files — for example, when applying a date for an event that occurred earlier. Choose from common formats such as DD/MM/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD, or use a custom pattern to match your workflow.

The tool also provides useful style options: you can change font size, color, and location (bottom-right, bottom-left, top-right, top-left or center). For improved readability over complex backgrounds, enable the semi-transparent box or drop shadow behind the text. If you need extra space at the bottom — for captions, notes, or printing margins — the “Add extra white space” option appends a clean white strip to each image and positions the date above it. This is handy when preparing images for physical albums or adding handwritten notes after printing.

A typical workflow: upload your photos, choose date source (today/custom), select a format and style, select “Add extra white space” if needed, then click “Process Images.” Review previews on the right, make adjustments (font size or color) if necessary, then download an individual image or click “Download All (ZIP)” to get everything in a single archive. Processing runs locally in your browser and is fast for most photo batches. For very large batches (hundreds of high-resolution images), processing time depends on your machine’s CPU and memory — the tool shows live progress to keep you informed.

Why add a date watermark rather than using file timestamps? File metadata (created or modified dates) may change when files are edited, copied, or moved and can be unreliable for long-term evidence. A visible, burned-in date on each image ensures the displayed date persists through sharing, printing, or format conversion. However, for strict legal use, investigate local rules for admissible evidence — some use cryptographic signing or tamper-evident systems.

This JDIRO tool is designed for everyday users and professionals alike. It is free, requires no sign-in, and is built with accessibility and responsive layout in mind. If you need extra automation (for example, reading EXIF timestamps from each photo and using them automatically), consider advanced options that read metadata — this version focuses on a straightforward, fast, manual approach so you remain in control of the applied date.

In short, bulk date stamping saves time, standardizes records and prepares images for archiving, printing, and sharing. Use the customization options to match your visual standards and workflow, and enjoy the convenience of downloading a ready-to-store ZIP of dated images.

FAQ

Can I use today's date or a custom date?
Yes — choose “Today's date” to apply the current date automatically, or pick “Custom date” and enter the date you want applied to all images.
Does the tool upload images to your server?
No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Files are not sent to our servers unless you explicitly upload them elsewhere.
Can I stamp images with the date from their EXIF metadata?
This version applies a single date (today or custom) to all images. EXIF-based stamping is possible but would be a separate mode that reads embedded timestamps per file.
How do I download all processed images?
Use the “Download All (ZIP)” button — processed images will be compressed into a ZIP file and downloaded to your device.
