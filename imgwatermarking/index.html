Why Use an Image Watermarker?

With content theft on the rise, watermarks act as a simple yet effective layer of security. Adding your name, logo, or copyright text to your images:

  • Helps with brand visibility and recognition
  • Maintains professional presentation
  • Establishes ownership of your work
