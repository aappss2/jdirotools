Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is a Mutual Fund Calculator?

It helps you estimate potential returns from SIP or lumpsum investments over a chosen period at an assumed rate.

2. Are the returns guaranteed?

No. Mutual fund returns are market-linked and not guaranteed. The calculator gives an estimate based on past averages.

3. What’s better: SIP or Lumpsum?

SIP is better for disciplined, long-term investing; lumpsum is suitable if you have idle funds and market timing works in your favor.
