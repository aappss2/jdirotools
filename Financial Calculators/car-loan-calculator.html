📊 How Does It Work?

The EMI is calculated using the formula:

EMI = [P × r × (1+r)n] / [(1+r)n - 1]

Where:

  • P = Loan Amount
  • r = Monthly Interest Rate
  • n = Number of Months

This ensures accuracy and transparency in your loan planning.
