About Immediate Annuities

An immediate annuity is a financial product that converts a lump sum investment into a stream of regular payments, usually starting immediately after purchase. It is commonly used to secure a stable income during retirement, helping you manage expenses and maintain financial security.

Immediate annuities are available in various types, including fixed, variable, and inflation-adjusted annuities. Fixed immediate annuities provide a guaranteed payout, while variable annuities allow investment growth but come with more risk. Inflation-adjusted annuities help preserve purchasing power over time.

Using this calculator, you can estimate your expected payouts based on your investment amount, interest rate, and payout duration, enabling smarter retirement planning. Consult a financial advisor to select the annuity type that fits your goals.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: What is an immediate annuity?
An immediate annuity is a contract with an insurance company where you pay a lump sum and receive regular payments immediately or within a short period.

Q2: How is the payout calculated?
The payout is calculated based on the investment amount, interest rate, and duration of payments. Our calculator uses a standard formula for fixed annuities to give an estimate.

Q3: Can the payments be monthly instead of yearly?
Yes, many annuities offer monthly payments. You can adjust the interest rate and duration to a monthly basis to estimate monthly payouts.

Q4: Are immediate annuities safe?
Generally, immediate annuities from reputable insurance companies are considered safe. However, returns are subject to the terms of the contract, and inflation may reduce purchasing power over time.

Q5: Do I get my investment back?
Some immediate annuities provide payments for a fixed period or lifetime but may not return the full initial investment unless structured with a guaranteed period or death benefit.
