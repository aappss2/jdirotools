📊 Formula for Home Loan EMI

The EMI is calculated using the formula:

EMI = [P × r × (1+r)n] / [(1+r)n - 1]

Where:

  • P = Loan Amount
  • r = Monthly Interest Rate
  • n = Loan Tenure in Months

📝 Example

Suppose you borrow ₹40,00,000 at an annual interest rate of 8% for 20 years. The EMI will be calculated instantly by the tool, showing you the monthly amount you must pay.
