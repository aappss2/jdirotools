Time Zone Converter — Convert Times Instantly

Our Time Zone Converter helps you quickly convert dates and times between any IANA time zones (for example, America/Los_Angeles, Europe/London, Asia/Kolkata). It automatically accounts for daylight saving time (DST) and shows timezone abbreviations and offsets, so you'll know the exact local time anywhere in the world.

How to use

  1. Select a source date and time using the Date & Time picker (or click "Convert Using Current Time").
  2. Choose the source time zone and a target time zone. Optionally add more target zones separated by commas.
  3. Click Convert to see converted times immediately. Copy or download results as needed.

Why this tool is useful

  • Plan meetings across time zones easily
  • Schedule events and avoid DST confusion
  • Convert timestamps when collaborating with remote teams
