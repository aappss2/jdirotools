Calorie Calculator – Complete Guide

Managing body weight effectively begins with understanding how many calories your body needs each day. Calories are units of energy, and your daily calorie requirement depends on several factors including age, gender, body composition, and physical activity level.

What Is BMR?

BMR, or Basal Metabolic Rate, represents the number of calories your body burns at complete rest to maintain vital functions such as breathing, circulation, and cell production. This calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, which is widely considered the most accurate formula for estimating BMR in adults.

What Is TDEE?

Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) expands on BMR by accounting for physical activity. By multiplying BMR by an activity factor, the calculator estimates how many calories you burn in an average day.

Weight Loss, Maintenance, and Gain

Once TDEE is calculated, calorie goals can be adjusted:

  • Weight loss: ~500 calorie deficit per day
  • Maintenance: Eat at TDEE
  • Weight gain: ~500 calorie surplus per day

These values align with evidence-based nutrition guidelines and support gradual, sustainable changes.

Macronutrient Breakdown

Calories alone are not enough. The calculator also provides a balanced macronutrient split:

  • Protein: ~25%
  • Carbohydrates: ~50%
  • Fats: ~25%

This distribution supports muscle maintenance, energy levels, and hormonal balance for most individuals.

Who Should Use This Tool?

  • People planning weight loss or gain
  • Fitness enthusiasts and athletes
  • Diet planners and nutrition students
  • Anyone seeking healthier eating habits

Accuracy & Limitations

This calculator provides scientifically grounded estimates. However, actual calorie needs may vary due to genetics, metabolism, medical conditions, and lifestyle factors.
