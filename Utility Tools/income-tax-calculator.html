📌 What is Income Tax?

Income tax is a direct tax levied by the government on the income earned by individuals, Hindu Undivided Families (HUFs), companies, and other entities. In India, income tax is collected by the Income Tax Department under the Ministry of Finance.

📊 Income Tax Slabs in India (FY 2024-25)

  • Old Regime: Higher exemptions and deductions but higher tax rates.
  • New Regime: Lower tax rates but fewer deductions.

💡 Key Deductions under Old Regime

  • Section 80C: Investments in PPF, ELSS, LIC, EPF, etc. (Limit: ₹1.5 lakh)
  • Section 80D: Health insurance premium deduction
  • HRA & LTA: House Rent Allowance and Leave Travel Allowance exemptions

🌍 Why Use an Income Tax Calculator?

❓ FAQs

Q. Which is better: Old regime or New regime?
It depends on your income and deductions. If you claim many deductions, the Old regime may benefit you; otherwise, the New regime may be better. [Source]

Q. Do I need to file ITR if income is below ₹2.5 lakh?
