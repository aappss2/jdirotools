Trigonometry Basics & Uses

Our trigonometry calculator is a quick sin cos tan calculator and online trigonometry solver for angles in degrees or radians. Trigonometry links triangle angles to side ratios: sine (opposite/hypotenuse), cosine (adjacent/hypotenuse), and tangent (opposite/adjacent). Their reciprocals—cosecant, secant, and cotangent—extend the toolkit, while inverse functions recover angles from ratios. In practice, these tools power navigation bearings, signal processing phase shifts, game and AR camera rotations, civil engineering slopes, and satellite dish alignment. Enter an angle, pick a mode, then tap a function to see precise results. This compact math tool is optimized for mobile and desktop, with accessible labels and semantic HTML to help learners and professionals get answers fast.
