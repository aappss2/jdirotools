What is GPA?

GPA stands for Grade Point Average. It is a measure of a student’s academic performance during a specific semester or academic term. GPA is calculated by dividing the total grade points earned by the total number of credit hours attempted. A higher GPA indicates better performance in your subjects.

What is CGPA?

CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average. It represents the overall academic performance across multiple semesters or years. Unlike GPA, which is calculated for a single term, CGPA takes into account all subjects and semesters to give you a complete picture of your academic progress.
