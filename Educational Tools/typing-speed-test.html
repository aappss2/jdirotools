Free Online Typing Speed Test Tool

Welcome to our free Typing Speed Test Tool, designed to help you measure your typing speed and accuracy in real-time. Why Use a Typing Speed Test?

Typing is one of the most valuable digital skills in today’s world. A faster and more accurate typing speed not only saves time but also increases productivity. Students can complete assignments quicker, professionals can draft emails efficiently, and programmers can code without unnecessary pauses. By regularly testing your typing skills, you can identify your current level and focus on areas of improvement.

Features of Our Typing Speed Test Tool

  • Multiple Durations: Choose from 15, 30, 60, or 120 seconds, or set your own custom time limit.
  • Custom Passages: Select from random sentences, classic paragraphs, code snippets, or paste your own text to practice with.
  • Live Statistics: Get real-time updates on your Words Per Minute (WPM), Characters Per Minute (CPM), Accuracy, and Error count.

  • Progress Bar & Timer: Visual progress tracking to keep you motivated during practice.
  • History & Export: Your test results are stored locally in your browser. You can review past results, copy them, or export them as CSV files.

How to Use the Tool

  1. Select a time duration for your test.
  2. Choose a passage or paste your own custom text.
  3. Start typing in the input area — the timer begins automatically.
  4. Check your live results such as WPM, accuracy, and errors as you type.
  5. When the timer ends, review your results and save them to your history.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What does WPM mean?

WPM stands for Words Per Minute. It measures how many words you can type in one minute, assuming five characters per word.

2. How is accuracy calculated?

Accuracy is calculated by comparing the number of correct characters to the total characters you typed during the test.

3. Can I paste my own text for practice?

Yes, simply select Paste Your Own in the passage dropdown and enter your custom text to practice typing with it.

4. Is my typing history saved online?

No, your history is saved only in your browser’s local storage, ensuring privacy and security.

5. Does the tool work on mobile devices?

Yes, the tool is mobile-friendly and works smoothly on smartphones, tablets, and desktops without any installation required.
