Quick formulas

  • Square: A = a²
  • Rectangle: A = w × h
  • Circle: A = πr²
  • Triangle: A = ½ × base × height
  • Trapezoid: A = ½ × (a + b) × h
  • Parallelogram: A = base × height
  • Ellipse: A = πab (a,b are semi-axes)
  • Regular polygon: A = (n × s²) / (4 × tan(π/n))
