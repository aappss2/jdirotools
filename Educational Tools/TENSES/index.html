What is a TENSE? 🤔

Definition

A tense is a verb form that shows the time of an action or state.

Present Simple Tense

Formula

Subject + V1 (+s/es) + Object

Definition

Expresses habitual actions, general truths, or facts

Usage

  • Daily habits and routines
  • Universal truths and facts
  • Scheduled future events

Examples

  • ✅ He plays cricket every day.
  • ❌ Riya does not eat mangoes.
  • ❓ Do you like pizza?
  • 📖 The sun rises in the east.
  • 📚 They read books daily.

Common Error

❌ He play cricket. → ✅ He plays cricket.

Mini Practice

1. I _____ (read) books daily.

2. _____ Ram (work) here?

3. Birds _____ (fly) in the sky.

Teacher Tip: Emphasize the 's/es' rule for third person singular!

Present Continuous Tense

Formula

Subject + am/is/are + V1-ing + Object

Definition

Ongoing actions happening now

Usage

  • Actions happening at the moment of speaking
  • Temporary situations
  • Future plans

Examples

  • ✅ She is dancing beautifully.
  • ❌ They are not coming today.
  • ❓ Are you studying now?
  • 📺 I am watching TV.
  • 🏃 My friends are playing outside.

Common Error

❌ He is play cricket. → ✅ He is playing cricket.

Mini Practice

1. The children _____ (sing) songs.

2. She _____ (not/write) now.

3. _____ you (listen) to me?

Exam Alert: Remember: Stative verbs (know, like, believe) don't use continuous forms!

Present Perfect Tense

Formula

Subject + has/have + V3 + Object

Definition

Actions completed at an unspecified time in the past, connected to the present

Examples

  • I have finished my homework.
  • She has not visited Agra.
  • Have you seen my bag?

Common Error

❌ She have gone. → ✅ She has gone.

Special Notes & Exceptions ⚠️

Will vs Going to

WILL

  • Instant decisions
  • Promises
  • Predictions

I will answer the phone.

GOING TO

  • Planned actions
  • Things about to happen
  • Evidence-based predictions

I am going to start a new hobby.

Stative Verbs

Verbs like know, believe, love, own, need don't appear in continuous tenses

✅ I have known her for years.

❌ I have been knowing her for years.

Since vs For

Since: Specific starting point (since 2012)

For: Length of time (for 5 years)

Practice Exercise 1: Fill in the Blanks 📝

  1. She _____ (go) to school every day.
  2. I _____ (watch) TV when the phone rang.
  3. They _____ (finish) their lunch before we arrived.
  4. He _____ (study) for two hours when his friend called.
  5. The train _____ (leave) at 8 pm tonight.

Answers:

  1. goes (Present Simple)
  2. was watching (Past Continuous)
  3. had finished (Past Perfect)
  4. had been studying (Past Perfect Continuous)
  5. will leave (Future Simple)

Practice Exercise 2: Identify the Tense 🔍

  1. "She is cooking dinner." - Tense: _____
  2. "I had finished my homework." - Tense: _____
  3. "They will be waiting for you." - Tense: _____
  4. "You have been working hard." - Tense: _____
  5. "He writes beautiful poems." - Tense: _____

Answers:

  1. Present Continuous
  2. Past Perfect
  3. Future Continuous
  4. Present Perfect Continuous
  5. Present Simple

Exam Tips for Success 🎯

📖 Reported Speech

Present → Past

Example: He says, "I am tired." → He said that he was tired.

🔀 Conditionals

  • First: If + present, will + V1
  • Second: If + past, would + V1
  • Third: If + past perfect, would have + V3

✏️ Editing Strategy

  • Check subject-verb agreement
  • Match time expressions with tenses
  • Verify sequence of events
  • Look for stative verb errors

Golden Rules Checklist ⭐

1

Always match subject and verb

2

Use time words (yesterday, now, tomorrow) carefully

3

Never use continuous forms with stative verbs

4

Use present in 'if/when' clauses for future meaning

5

For and since: for (length of time), since (starting point)

Quick Reference

Present: Now
Past: Before now
Future: After now
Simple: Complete
Continuous: Ongoing
Perfect: Completed before a time
