Pregnancy Weeks & Due Date Explained

Pregnancy is counted in weeks, usually starting from the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP). However, sometimes you may only know your estimated due date (EDD). That’s why pregnancy calculators work both ways: LMP → Weeks Pregnant and Due Date → Weeks Pregnant.

Two Ways to Track Pregnancy

  • LMP Method: Count 40 weeks from your last period to estimate due date.
  • Due Date Method: Count backwards 40 weeks from your due date to estimate start of pregnancy.

Why This Matters

Tracking pregnancy weeks helps doctors monitor growth, schedule ultrasounds, and ensure the right prenatal care at each stage. It also helps expectant parents prepare for birth.

FAQs

Q: Which method is more accurate?
A: Ultrasound in early pregnancy provides the most accurate dating, especially with irregular cycles.

Q: What if my due date doesn’t match my LMP?
A: Doctors may adjust based on ultrasound results.

Q: How many weeks is full-term pregnancy?
A: About 37–42 weeks is considered full-term.
