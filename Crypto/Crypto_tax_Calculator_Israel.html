Cryptocurrency Tax Israel 2026 – Complete ITA Guide

Cryptocurrency tax Israel rules are governed by the Israel Tax Authority (ITA). Under israel crypto regulations 2026, crypto assets such as Bitcoin are classified as property rather than currency. This classification means capital gains tax generally applies when individuals sell crypto at a profit.

Legal Status of Cryptocurrency in Israel

Cryptocurrency is legal in Israel. However, it is regulated for tax and anti-money laundering purposes. The ITA considers digital assets as taxable assets, not foreign currency.

Capital Gains Crypto Israel

Individual investors typically pay 25% capital gains tax on net profit. Net gain equals sale proceeds minus purchase cost and trading fees.

Business Crypto Taxation

Businesses engaged in frequent trading may be taxed under income tax rules. Rates depend on corporate or individual marginal tax rates, typically ranging from 10% to 50%.

Mining and Staking

Mining and staking rewards may be taxed as business income at receipt and later subject to capital gains upon disposal.

Reporting and Compliance

Crypto transactions must be reported in annual tax returns. Failure to report may trigger audits, penalties, and interest charges.

Loss Carry-Forward

Crypto losses may offset future capital gains. Proper documentation is essential for compliance with israel tax authority crypto regulations.

VAT Considerations

Businesses may face VAT implications depending on activity classification.
