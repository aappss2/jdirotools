Accessibility Service Disclosure

Reels Counter uses Android's Accessibility Service to detect swipe gestures while you browse short-form videos (such as Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts). This allows the app to automatically count reels viewed and track scrolling activity.

What Data Is Accessed

  • Swipe and scrolling gestures within supported apps.
  • Information necessary to determine when a reel or short has been viewed.

What Data Is NOT Collected

  • Messages, chats, emails, or text you type.
  • Passwords, payment information, or personal credentials.
  • Photos, videos, contacts, or other personal files.

How Your Data Is Used

  • To count reels viewed and generate usage statistics.
  • To provide screen-time and activity tracking features.

Data Handling

  • All data is stored locally on your device.
  • No personal data is transmitted to external servers.
Consent: By enabling Accessibility Service, you acknowledge and consent to this functionality.
