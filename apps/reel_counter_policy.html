Privacy Policy

Reels Counter takes your privacy seriously.

1. Data Collection

We operate on an offline-first architecture. All watched reels, time tracking sessions, and scroll configurations are stored entirely inside your local Room SQL Database. We do not transmit your personal data to remote servers.

2. Permissions & Accessibility

We use the Android Accessibility Service strictly to capture vertical page/swipe gestures in social media scrolling video packages (such as Instagram and YouTube Shorts) to calculate reels counts automatically. We do not extract text fields, read direct messages, or harvest passwords.

3. Accessibility Compliance

Our service functions purely on local devices and complies fully with the Google Play Store Developer Content Policy guidelines.
