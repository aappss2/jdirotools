How Does the Coin Flip Algorithm Work?

The coin-flip logic is based entirely on randomness generated by JavaScript’s secure Math.random() function. This ensures that each flip has an equal chance of appearing as heads or tails unless you adjust the bias manually. By setting a bias percentage, you can control the probability of heads, which is incredibly useful for probability studies and simulations. The outcome is displayed using a 3D animation that rotates around the coin’s center, making the final face appear naturally based on your chosen settings.
