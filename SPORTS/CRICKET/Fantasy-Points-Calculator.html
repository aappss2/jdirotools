Understanding Fantasy Cricket Points

Fantasy cricket has revolutionized how fans engage with the sport. Fantasy points determine how well your selected players perform in your fantasy team. Although point systems vary by platform (like Dream11, My11Circle, or FanFight), a common calculation is:

  • Batting: 1 point per run, bonus points for milestones (50s, 100s).
  • Bowling: 25 points per wicket, extra for 3-wicket and 5-wicket hauls.
  • Fielding: 10 points per catch or run-out/stumping.

Our calculator uses a simplified version: Points = Runs × 1 + Wickets × 25 + Catches × 10.

Fantasy Points in Different Roles

  • Batsmen: Earn most points from runs, strike rate, and milestones.
  • Bowlers: Gain big from wickets, economy rate, and special bonuses.
  • All-rounders: Valuable because they contribute in both batting and bowling.
  • Wicketkeepers: Earn through runs, catches, and stumpings.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How are fantasy cricket points calculated?

It depends on the platform, but generally runs, wickets, catches, strike rates, and economy rates contribute to points.

2. Which players score the most fantasy points?

All-rounders usually score the most because they contribute in both batting and bowling.

3. How many points is a wicket worth in fantasy cricket?

On average, 25 points per wicket, with extra bonuses for 3 or 5 wickets.

4. Do catches and stumpings count for fantasy points?

Yes, most platforms award around 10 points per catch/stumping.

5. Can I use this calculator for IPL and World Cup fantasy leagues?

Yes, though scoring systems may vary slightly. Adjust the formula based on platform rules.
