Understanding OPS in Baseball

On-base Plus Slugging (OPS) is a key statistic in baseball that combines a player's ability to get on base (OBP) with their power hitting (SLG). It provides a more comprehensive measure of offensive performance than batting average alone.

OBP = (H + BB + HBP) ÷ (AB + BB + HBP + SF)

SLG = (1B + 2×2B + 3×3B + 4×HR) ÷ AB

OPS = OBP + SLG

By calculating OPS, coaches, analysts, and fantasy baseball players can evaluate a player's overall offensive contribution efficiently. A higher OPS indicates a player who both gets on base frequently and hits for power.
