Understanding Field Goal Percentage in Basketball

Field Goal Percentage (FG%) is a key statistic in basketball that measures a player’s shooting accuracy. It is calculated by dividing the total number of field goals made by the total number of field goals attempted and multiplying by 100 to express it as a percentage.

A higher FG% indicates a player is more accurate in scoring, which is valuable for team success. Coaches and analysts use this metric to evaluate efficiency, player performance, and scoring consistency. FG% can also help in comparing players and setting benchmarks in competitive basketball.

For example, if a player makes 9 field goals out of 15 attempts, their FG% would be (9 ÷ 15) × 100 = 60%. Our interactive Field Goal Percentage Calculator allows players, coaches, and fans to quickly determine shooting accuracy, aiding performance evaluation and strategy development.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Field Goal Percentage?

FG% measures the ratio of field goals made to field goals attempted, expressed as a percentage.

How is it calculated?

Field Goal Percentage = (Field Goals Made ÷ Field Goals Attempted) × 100

What is a good FG%?

A FG% above 50% is considered very good, 45%-50% is average, and below 40% is below average.
