Find & Replace Text Online (Regex Supported)

Clean up text, refactor code, or edit large documents in seconds. J1RO’s Find & Replace tool runs fully in your browser, supports regex and whole-word matching, preserves case, and offers live preview.

What you can do

  • Regex search with groups ($1, $2) and named groups ($<name>)
  • Whole words, case sensitive, multiline and dotall (match across lines)
  • Preserve case in replacements (UPPER, lower, Title)
  • Selection-only replace, highlight matches, next/prev navigation
  • Batch replace with simple rules (one per line: find => replace)
  • Open/save files, copy to clipboard, and see live stats
  • Private by design: processing is client‑side, no uploads

How to use (step‑by‑step)

  1. Paste or load your text file.
  2. Enter your Find pattern and optional replacement text.
  3. Toggle Regex, Case, Whole words, Multiline, or Dotall as needed.
  4. Click Next/Prev to jump between matches, or Replace/Replace All.
  5. Use Batch Replace for multiple rules at once.

Useful regex examples

1) Collapse multiple spaces to one

Find (regex): \s{2,}
Replace: ␣ (single space)

2) Trim trailing spaces at line ends

Find (regex, multiline): [ \t]+$
Replace: (leave empty)

3) Reformat YYYY-MM-DD to DD/MM/YYYY

Find (regex): (\d{4})-(\d{2})-(\d{2})
Replace: $3/$2/$1

4) Wrap emails in < >

Find (regex): [\w.+-]+@[\w.-]+\.\w+
Replace: <$&>

5) Convert snake_case words to Title (basic)

Find (regex): \b([a-z]+)_([a-z]+)\b
Replace: $1 $2
Tip: Then enable "Preserve case" and title-case manually or run a second pass.

Tips for accuracy

  • Use Whole words to avoid matching inside larger tokens.
  • To match across lines, enable Dotall or use [\s\S].
  • Use Preserve case when replacing names or titles.
  • Batch rules run top‑to‑bottom; place specific rules before general ones.

Privacy & performance

Your text stays on your device. For very large inputs, preview may be limited for speed, but replace operations still work.

FAQ: Find & Replace Text Tool

Does this tool upload my text?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

How do capture groups work ($1, $2, $<name>)?

Enable Regex. Use parentheses to capture parts in your Find pattern. Reference them in Replace with $1, $2, etc., or named groups like (?<user>...) and $<user>.

What do Multiline and Dotall mean?

Multiline makes ^ and $ match the start/end of each line. Dotall lets . match newline characters (search across lines).

What does Whole words do?

It adds word boundaries (\b) so matches only full words, not substrings inside larger words.

How does Preserve case work?

It adapts your replacement to the matched case: UPPERCASE, lowercase, or Titlecase (first letter capitalized).

Can I replace only in a selection?

Yes. Enable Selection only and highlight the target text in the editor. Replace operations will be confined to that selection.

How do I escape special regex characters?

Click the Esc button next to Find to auto‑escape the current query, or disable Regex mode.

How do Batch Replace rules work?

Enter one rule per line in the form find => replace. Toggle Regex/Whole words/Case as needed. Rules are applied in order from top to bottom.

Is there a size limit?

The tool handles large text. For very large inputs, live preview may be limited for performance, but replace still works.
